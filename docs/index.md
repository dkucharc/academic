## About

Daniel Kucharczyk is an Assistant Professor with the Faculty of Pure and Applied Mathematics at Wrocław University of Technology. <br>

His research interests include the application of mathematics and AI/ML methods in:

- quantitative finance,
- remote sensing.

In addition, he has been actively working in the following fields:

- reproducible research,
- scientific computing,
- cloud software architecture.
